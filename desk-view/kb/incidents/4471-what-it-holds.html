The three problems

  1. The shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” This is the bug the #4471 page describes, and the queued fix.
  2. The booking-v3 form hint asks for MM/DD/YYYY. People type what the hint shows, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Only form reports from the three v3 tenants. The fix is the hint, not the parser.
  3. Invites move by one day. No swap: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” · “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” The desk already marked these as a one-day move in desk-notes.json and filed them under the parser by tenant, where they are still counted. A day/month fix cannot move a date by one day.

Why they were merged

Triage merges on subject (decision 0001). The 412 reports use twelve subjects, and the problems share them: “Date is wrong” and “Wrong day on the invite” each carry reports of all three. Subject matching cannot tell them apart. Intake’s tag does not help either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant’s cause on many reports (column “intake tag wrong”).
